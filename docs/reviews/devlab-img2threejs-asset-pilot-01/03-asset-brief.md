# Asset brief

`Cinder Relay Drone` is a compact industrial-military signal relay intended for
ash corridors, orbital ruins, and devastated infrastructure. It is autonomous,
field-repaired, permanently hovering, and functional rather than heroic.

The readable hierarchy is a faceted armored core, a broken upper relay arc,
unequal lateral stabilizers, a dim ember thermal core, paired cold sensors, an
underslung service module, one displaced access panel, and asymmetric service
marks. The silhouette and material system are original to this pilot.

The exclusions in the sprint brief are hard boundaries. No named franchise,
character, animal, Mint corpus asset, or upstream model is used as a visual
reference.

# Budget profiles

Four immutable version-1 profiles are registered: `tiny-prop-v1`, `small-prop-v1`, `medium-prop-v1`, and `large-prop-v1`. Exceeding a target yields OPTIONAL; exceeding a maximum yields REQUIRED; invalid geometry or a resource leak yields BLOCKER. Cinder passes `small-prop-v1` with 2,180 triangles, 10 draw calls, 5 materials, 0 asset textures and 14 nodes.

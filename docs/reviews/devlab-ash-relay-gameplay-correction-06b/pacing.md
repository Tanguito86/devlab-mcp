# Pacing

The final perfect-information bot duration is 115.5 seconds. Representative phase entry times are 6.033s Encounter 1, 15.167s checkpoint, 18.183s Encounter 2, 80.517s Calibration, 95.250s Overload, and 115.483s victory.

This is a deterministic lower bound, not a human 3-5 minute measurement. The implementation adds active onboarding, hatch reads, committed boss patterns, recoveries, and vulnerability windows. It does not slow the player, inflate HP, add infinite enemies, or insert passive doors. Human duration remains a reviewer measurement rather than an asserted builder PASS.
